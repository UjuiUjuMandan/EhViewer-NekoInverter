<p>This is a fork of EhViewer-NekoInverter that changes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Strips the original SNI host to bypass DPI. Uses hard-coded origin IPs and *.cdn.cloudflare.net suffix to circumvent DNS poisoning.</li>
<li>Filters galleries to NON-H for guests. Signed-in users are not subject to this filter.</li>
<li>Defaults to DIRECT connections rather than the system proxy.</li>
</ul>
