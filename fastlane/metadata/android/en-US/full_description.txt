<p>An E-Hentai Application for Android.</p>
<p>This fork of EhViewer-NekoInverter changes:</p>
<ul>
<li>For direct connections, strips the original SNI host. It uses hard-coded origin IPs for exhentai.org and its subdomains, and resolves e-hentai.org hosts through corresponding *.cdn.cloudflare.net names.</li>
<li>Defaults to the Non-H category for guests and filters popular and toplist results to Non-H for guests. Signed-in users are not subject to this filter.</li>
<li>Defaults to DIRECT connections rather than the system proxy. If you rely on a system proxy, select it in the app's proxy settings.</li>
</ul>
<p>TLS certificate verification is not disabled: the socket factory changes only the SNI host for direct connections; OkHttp's trust manager and hostname verifier remain unchanged.</p>
